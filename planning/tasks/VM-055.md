## Acceptance

- A --fp-on option sets CPACR_EL1 = 0x300000 on the boot vCPU with KVM_SET_ONE_REG before its first KVM_RUN and records it in the start event. Without the option, behaviour is unchanged.
- The reason is recorded with its measurement: XNU common_start (osfmk/arm64/start.s:762-763) writes CPACR_EL1 without an ISB, and the VM-054 probe fp-sync-cpu0/4/9 shows that an FP instruction after an unsynchronized FPEN write still traps on the S24 cores.
- check.py passes (ASan/UBSan, TSan, warning-free static NDK build). The limit is stated: PSCI CPU_ON secondaries are reset by KVM and do not get this state.
