- The upstream source (references/s24-port/xnu/hackios, loader_return.c and the redwood.patch hunks) is compared with the payload: the facts that hold in the payload (slot offset, reasons 0/1/2, loader_log size, loader_live magic, kernel-side requirements for the stub such as TCR T0SZ range) are separated from facts that are only in the source.
