## Acceptance

- Queues and the job table are fixed-size, and a full queue refuses work without any hardware effect.
- Work starts only after its waits finish, and retirements reach the core in an order where dependents follow what they wait for.
- A counter value that cannot be true faults the device and retires nothing; a hung head times out, and only proof of quiescence releases running work.
- Tests run against the real GPU-013 core and under ASan/UBSan and TSan; single-line mutants are killed.
- No register, firmware, IRQ or ring constant is invented, and nothing claims GPU execution.
