## Acceptance

- Sanitized dated captures (no serials, IMEIs, MACs, accounts) from the connected S24 through ADB/su read-only commands: dwc3/usbdrd/eusb/usbdp/repeater lines of dmesg, /proc/iomem entries for 0x17820000-0x1790ffff, the HSI0 clock and USB regulator lines of the clk and regulator summaries, /sys/class/udc and the controller debugfs regdump when it exists. No write to any node, no settings change.
- Each captured value is compared with the vendor-source expectation of NAT-040 (controller version, speed, clock rates, PHY version, repeater address) and every mismatch is listed; the controller base 0x17900000 and the HSI0 sysreg base are confirmed or corrected.
- If the phone is not attached the task states that as its only blocker; it is resumed when `adb devices` lists the S24.

## Steps

1. Wait for the device and capture read-only
2. Sanitize and compare with the source plan
