## Acceptance

- The inspector opens a zip with zipfile only (no extraction by default), parses BuildManifest.plist (plistlib), and reports ProductBuildVersion, ProductVersion, SupportedProductTypes, each BuildIdentity&#x27;s device class and the manifest paths of OS/SystemOS/AppOS/Cryptex disk images, DeviceTree and KernelCache; with --sha256 it hashes members by streaming.

- Host tests on synthetic zips built in a temp dir (accepted case, each rejecting case, zip-slip member names refused, absent file, a member listed but absent); no Apple data is invented as a fixture beyond the minimum manifest keys, and the test says so.
