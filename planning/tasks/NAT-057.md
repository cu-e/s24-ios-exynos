## Acceptance

- A session driver for the one image loader-diag-xnu-ckpt3-usb derived from docs/procedures/nat-049/session_usblog.py: pinned RECOVERY/Image.payload hashes of the NAT-052 package, a decision-file gate (exactly one docs/decisions/*-nat052-loader-diag-xnu-ckpt3-usb-single-run-*.md naming both hashes and decision 0019), a registry gate (native permit citing that file; NAT-039, NAT-046, NAT-043 and NAT-052 done), the udev gate, collector and watcher self-tests, one-shot marker, full-hash write and readback of RECOVERY only, restore of the pinned stock image, one reboot. Host tests with a fake adb and fake collector prove: no device command before every gate is open, a failed write/readback/watcher/collector restores and never reboots, the marker prevents a second run, and mutation testing of the gates leaves no survivor without a documented equivalent.
- An analyser that applies the NAT-049 archive integrity, T0, boot-id and USB-log coverage rules by importing docs/procedures/nat-049/analyze_usblog.py (read only: NAT-055 owns edits to it)  and then reads the CK notes (decode52.progress): PASS only for a live archive with one boot of this image, consecutive notes, the final stop or the announced HOLD, no drops; PARTIAL names the last stop; EXCEPTION carries esr/elr/far; FAIL and undetermined as in the decision draft of NAT-052. Tested on archives produced by the NAT-052 simulation (full, exception, short, engine-down, corrupted); a synthetic archive never yields a plain PASS.
- No device access, no native run and no claim of hardware behaviour; the driver is not executed against the phone and its preflight is not run before NAT-043 is done.
