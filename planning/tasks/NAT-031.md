## Acceptance

- A deterministic hash-anchored loader variant fills slot [1]=stub PA, [2]=checkpoint, [3]=PA-VA (found by scanning the copied kernel segments for the magic, refusing unless exactly one 8-byte-aligned match followed by three zero words), keeps the kernel&#x27;s own entry word (no entry stub), cleans the window after the fill and jumps to the real entry; every earlier image stays byte-identical.
- The return stub handles reason 2 (assembly and C checkpoint) and reason 1 (early exception) and a held final stop: clears SCTLR_EL1 M/C, ic iallu, sets its own stack, draws new marker frames carrying reason, code, ESR/ELR/FAR where present and the log length, and never touches memory other than its own stack and the framebuffer/DECON registers already granted. Host checks: decoder-based interpretation of the stub, independent frame decode, alignment scan, reproducible build, package, gated session driver.
- An image-specific decision request (SHA-256s, device access, what runs and what does not, risks, recovery, Not granted) is put to the owner. Nothing is flashed, run on the phone or jumped to before that decision.
