## Acceptance

- Before the mount: /proc/mounts and /sys/kernel/debug are recorded. The only state change is `mount -t debugfs none /sys/kernel/debug`, undone by `umount` in the same session; afterwards /proc/mounts has no debugfs line again (verified and recorded), and getenforce, sys.usb.config and adb connectivity are unchanged.
- Only these debugfs files are read: regulator/regulator_summary and pm_genpd/pm_genpd_summary (software state), and clk/clk_summary last, under a 20 s timeout, because rate recalculation may read CMU registers (stop and record if it does not return). No regdump file, no gpio file, no write to any debugfs node.
- The sanitized extract is compared with the NAT-039 comparison rows C11d and C13 (clock gates/rates of the HSI0 and USB clocks, pd_hsi0 on/off); the evidence says these are Android-state values, not loader-entry state.

## Steps

1. Record mounts, mount debugfs, read the three summaries with the timeout, unmount, verify
2. Extend the NAT-039 comparison rows and write evidence.json
