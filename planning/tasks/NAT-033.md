## Acceptance

- The SIMD instruction forms that the kernel executes between C checkpoint 10 and C checkpoint 11 (cpu_data_init, PE_init_platform(0, boot_args) and what they call) are implemented in a subclass of the NAT-030 interpreter (nat-030 files unchanged) and every implemented form is checked against Unicorn on randomized register and memory inputs; forms outside that list still fail loudly.
- With them, the model runs the real kernel from the entry with a stop at asm checkpoint 5, the NAT-032 resume (slot[2] = 11, MMU-off branch to site5 - 28) and a C stop at checkpoint 11: either the C stop at 11 arrives (state at the arrival: registers, SCTLR/TCR/TTBR0/TTBR1/VBAR, the private block, loader_log length) or the first unmodelled or faulting instruction is named.
