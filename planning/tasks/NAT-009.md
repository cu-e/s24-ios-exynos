## Acceptance

- Integrate the NAT-007 freestanding S5E9945 watchdog arm/pet/disarm sequence into the E1S RECOVERY loader before XNU handoff so any hang in loader_main, loader_exception or early XNU triggers a bounded hardware SoC reset.
- Verify from Samsung kernel/S-Boot sources and PMU syscon offsets how SEC_RESET_REASON_RECOVERY (0x12345604 in PMU panic_inform) interacts with watchdog reset and ensure the loader clears any sticky recovery reboot marker before jumping to XNU.
- Rebuild and host-verify the updated RECOVERY image with zero C object R_AARCH64_ABS64 relocations.

## Steps

1. Consume NAT-007 watchdog register and PMU syscon definitions
2. Patch the E1S loader to arm the hardware watchdog and clear sticky recovery reboot markers
3. Verify with host tests and rebuild the NAT-006 RECOVERY image
